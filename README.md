# 🌊 PRAVAAH

### Predict. Prepare. Protect.

**PRAVAAH** is a real-time **Disaster Intelligence & Early Warning Platform** designed to help citizens and disaster-management authorities detect emerging risks, verify ground-level incidents, understand *why* an area is at risk, and take action before conditions become critical.

> **Together for a Safer Tomorrow.**

🌐 **Live Application:** https://pravaah-delta.vercel.app/

---

## 🚨 The Problem

During disasters, information rarely arrives in one clean place.

Authorities may have:

- 🌧️ Weather and rainfall information
- 🌊 River-level or sensor readings
- 📢 Citizen reports
- 📍 Geographic information
- ⚠️ Emergency alerts

But these signals can remain disconnected.

A citizen may report flooding before official systems recognize the severity.  
A sensor may detect worsening conditions without enough ground-level context.  
Authorities may know that an area is dangerous without immediately knowing **why the risk increased or what action should be taken next**.

PRAVAAH attempts to connect these pieces into a single disaster-intelligence workflow.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

PRAVAAH combines:

**Environmental Data + Sensor Intelligence + Citizen Reports + Officer Verification + Risk Analysis**

to generate a continuously updated picture of disaster risk.

Instead of only showing raw numbers, PRAVAAH answers three important questions:

### 1️⃣ What is happening?
Live rainfall, river levels, citizen incidents and ward-level conditions are monitored.

### 2️⃣ Where is the danger?
Each monitored ward receives a dynamic **Risk Score from 0–100**.

### 3️⃣ What should we do?
The platform explains contributing risk factors and provides recommended actions to support faster decision-making.
